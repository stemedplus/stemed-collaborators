Help people find you

For individuals. Every STEMEd+ member has a card, made from their STEMEd+ Commons profile. Your interests are what people search for.

  1. New to STEMEd+? Join STEMEd+ first. Sign in through your institution, ORCID, or another provider.
  2. Sign in and open your profile. Choose Edit profile.
  3. Add Academic interests: your topics, plus skills and methods.
    For example: "science identity", "qualitative interviews", "psychometrics", "teacher professional learning".
  4. Say what you can offer and what you need: add interests that start with Offer: or Need:.
    For example: "Offer: qualitative coding", "Offer: K-12 classroom access", "Need: psychometrics help". They show on your card as "I can offer" and "I'm looking for".
  5. Add a way to reach you, such as your website, email, or Mastodon, so people who find you can get in touch.
  6. Add the interest so you're listed here.
  7. Save. Your interests appear on your card within a day.

This site is part of STEMEd+ Commons, a Commons within Knowledge Commons. STEMEd+ Commons brings together teachers, researchers, and learning professionals from every field to share work, find each other, and build what comes next. As an open access platform, STEMEd+ Commons helps ensure STEM+ teaching and STEM+ education research are accessible for all people, in all communities.
